# Issues

Ranked. The builder works top to bottom and stops at the first line it cannot complete, noting why in QUESTIONS.md. Format, one issue per line: priority (P0 blocks the demo, P1 wrong behavior, P2 rough edge), spec file and section the change lives in, one sentence of the problem, one sentence of done. Example: P1 | specs/02-STUDENT-FRONT-DOOR.md Progress Screen Rules | In-reach outcomes show scores instead of bands | Progress shows a band against the level expectation and never a raw score.

P0 | specs/06-ENGINE.md Traceability | Engine outputs carry only a why string, so a content expert or system architect cannot see the evidence rows, settings, or graph edges behind a need, a proposal, a placed slot, an overnight move, or a level review flag | Every engine output stores a trace (evidence read, settings applied, edges walked, candidates considered and why each lost); an Engine Trace screen under the lead role shows the trace for any student or proposal, and every slot and proposal carries a "Why this" link that opens it.
P0 | specs/06-ENGINE.md Rules for proposeWorkshops | Proposals group students on the same lesson regardless of prerequisite mastery, can sit on a grouped student's placement, and never say where students go if the expert declines, so a math teacher cannot accept one with confidence | A group holds only students whose prerequisite lessons are mastered or who share the same prerequisite gap; a mixed group splits into two proposals; collisions list per student; no proposed time overlaps any grouped student's RW slot; a group below threshold becomes a 1:1 conference proposal; every proposal carries a one-line rationale a teacher would accept and a "what if I decline" line naming where each student goes instead.
P1 | specs/06-ENGINE.md Why Text | Why lines name a score but never its source or day, and a slot placed by walking the graph never names the edge | Every why names the specific evidence (source and day) and, where the graph was walked, the edge in plain words; a scenario samples 50 why lines and asserts each names an evidence source or a graph edge and contains none of behind, failing, at risk, deficient, low performer.
P1 | specs/06-ENGINE.md Graph in the Engine | In-reach items take the first serving lesson without checking prerequisites, open-slot ranking ignores the pathway graph for RW offerings, Pursue to Launch review does not read the Futre pathway, and walked edges are not recorded anywhere | needs() uses whatServes() and prefers serving lessons whose prerequisites the student has mastered; the ranker uses pathwaysNear() and gapsTo() for RW offerings; Pursue to Launch review uses gapsTo() on the Futre target pathway; walked edges are stored in the trace; the lead's Outcomes area has a graph browser that opens any node and a path finder showing the shortest builds_toward path between two outcomes.
P1 | specs/01-DATA-MODEL.md School Settings and specs/06-ENGINE.md Rules for buildWeek | Level targets exist in settings with the How Summit 3.0 Works defaults but cannot be edited on the Settings screen and buildWeek() does not read them | Settings edits the eight-type mix per level; buildWeek() and the ranker read them; Time and Talent compares actuals to these targets and says which types are outside ten points.
P2 | specs/10-KNOWLEDGE-GRAPH.md Build Sources and Fallbacks | Confirm the Learning Commons load is the real export at confidence 1.0, not the authored fallback | The Evidence Log and the graph browser show source learning_commons with the export version for every math progression edge, or DECISIONS.md records why the fetch failed and the authored edges stay.
