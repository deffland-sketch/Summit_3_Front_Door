# Issues

Ranked. The builder works top to bottom and stops at the first line it cannot complete, noting why in QUESTIONS.md. Format, one issue per line: priority (P0 blocks the demo, P1 wrong behavior, P2 rough edge), spec file and section the change lives in, one sentence of the problem, one sentence of done. Example: P1 | specs/02-STUDENT-HOMEBASE.md Progress Screen Rules | In-reach outcomes show scores instead of bands | Progress shows a band against the level expectation and never a raw score.
