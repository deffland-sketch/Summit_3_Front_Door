# Questions

Product questions met during the unattended build. Each one records the assumption taken and where in the code it lives. Nothing here blocked the build.

| # | Question | Assumption taken | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where does the family-call flag live? Spec 3 says a lead or case manager flags it; no object carries a flag. | A Touchpoint of kind `family` recorded by an adult with the case_manager or leader capacity, with a follow_up due today or later, is the flag. Marked private so only the mentor sees the note. | src/engine/flags.ts (`flagsFor`), seed/generate.ts (familyStudent) |
| 2 | Spec 1 says each student is 'at a different lesson from peers' and Spec 4 wants three workshop proposals with four or more students each. Both cannot hold at once. | Students cluster around two anchor lessons per math course (about two thirds at the anchor, the rest one lesson either side) so shared needs exist; the rest of the catalog position is per student. | seed/generate.ts (`courseAnchors`, `pos`) |
| 3 | Should buildWeek book a readiness assessment on its own (Spec 6 step 6) or wait for the mentor (Spec 3 Readiness screen)? | The engine places an assessment slot only when an assessment offering already exists for that student; the mentor's Readiness screen creates the offering. Technology recommends, the mentor books. | src/engine/build.ts step 6, src/engine/overnight.ts readiness branch |
| 4 | How many open slots does a guided student get per day? | Every block the engine did not fill is open; a guided student sees ranked options in each. The seed has guided students pick about three quarters of them on Friday so one or two stay open per day for the demo. | src/engine/build.ts step 8, seed/generate.ts (`fillRate`) |
| 5 | Do Expose students see Pursue offerings (college courses, placements) in Build My Week? | Yes, the full published menu is visible to every student; the ranking, not a gate, decides order. The coordinator places RW slots, so an Expose student cannot self-book a hospital rotation. | src/engine/rank.ts |
| 6 | What are the Year 1 experience-mix targets per level from How Summit 3.0 Works? | Resolved from the document itself: Year 1 (used for Expose and Explore) DI 15, PF 20, DD 10, IW 25, PP 5, RG 10, RW 5, WC 10; Pursue 10, 15, 10, 20, 5, 10, 25, 5; Launch 5, 5, 5, 10, 5, 10, 55, 5. Against them the seed lands within ten points on six types and misses on two: WC runs about 23% because a 45-minute morning community plus a 30-minute lunch is 18% of a seven-hour day, and DI runs near 0% because workshops form only from shared Required need (three to five a week across 100 students). A Year 1 DI target of 15% implies most students in a workshop most days, which the engine would reach only if workshops also formed on in-reach outcomes. Time and Talent shows the two misses rather than hiding them. | src/model/settings.ts (`DEFAULT_LEVEL_TARGETS`), src/screens/lead/TimeTalent.tsx |
| 7 | Launch students are mostly off campus. Do they still carry community circle and reflection slots in the plan? | Yes in v1: the week keeps the 8:30 circle and 15:00 reflection for everyone, and Today for a Launch student leads with the on-site schedule. A text check-in replacing the in-room reflection is a later refinement. | src/engine/build.ts steps 1 and 7 |
| 8 | Stored `why` lines are generated in English at build time. What does a Spanish-locale student see? | Screen chrome follows the locale; the stored why line stays in the build language. A structured why (key plus parameters) would let the UI translate it later. | src/copy/en.ts `why`, src/engine/needs.ts |
| 9 | Math standard names from Learning Commons are the full standard text, so some why lines run long. | The outcome name is the first sentence of the standard, cut at 96 characters. A hand-written short name per standard would read better on a phone. | src/graph/catalog.ts (`shortName`) |
| 10 | Which exact level names show on screen? | Expose, Explore, Pursue, Launch, title case, as in the specs. No grade levels anywhere. | src/copy/en.ts `app.levels` |
| 11 | Should the Spanish draft go in front of a student before review? | No. `es.ts` is marked `needs_review` and only loads when a student sets Spanish in Me; the landing page and adult screens stay English. A Spanish-speaking educator should read every line first. | src/copy/es.ts |
| 12 | Teal `#508278` on white is 4.37:1, under the 4.5:1 floor for body-size headings. | Headings use a slightly darker teal `#3f6a61` in light mode (indigo stays as the alternate); large headings still read as brand teal. Lighthouse accessibility on Today is 96 or above. | src/index.css `--color-heading` |
| 13 | What does the display board show for RW students off campus? | A second section, "Out in the world", lists each placement by start time and count with no student names; on-campus sessions show room and count. Never a program name, never a reason. | src/screens/lead/DisplayBoard.tsx |
| 14 | Who may approve a study group proposal? | The lead, from the Dashboard; approval writes a Group of kind study_group with the proposing student. Adding other members is a later screen. | src/screens/lead/Dashboard.tsx |
| 15 | Can a mentor book an assessment at a time that collides with a protected or RW slot? | No; the booking refuses and shows the no-options message. Open and chosen slots at that time are marked moved. | src/screens/mentor/Readiness.tsx |
| 16 | Is a Launch student's week realistic with the same community and reflection slots as everyone? | Kept for v1 (see question 7). Their Today leads with "On site today" and lists the one or two campus commitments underneath. | src/screens/student/Today.tsx |
| 17 | How should the engine treat a Required need the week already answered when new evidence arrives overnight? | Only needs whose evidence row arrived after publish produce a move; a need the published week already placed is left alone so the week holds. | src/engine/overnight.ts |
| 18 | The Sierra metrics (academic growth, belonging, ownership) have no data source. | Shown as placeholders labeled "survey, not yet connected" on the Model Dashboard, per Spec 5. | src/screens/lead/ModelDashboard.tsx |
| 19 | Should workshops form on in-reach outcomes as well as Required need, to reach the Year 1 direct-instruction target? | Not in v1; Spec 6 groups on Required need and needs_workshop only. Recorded as the lever that moves DI toward target. | src/engine/propose.ts |
| 20 | Should a student or a mentor see the "Why this" link that opens the lead's Engine Trace screen, or is the trace a lead and expert view only? | Shown everywhere in the sandbox: the visible why is a design principle and the trace is the long form of it. A role gate is one line in SlotCard if a school wants it. | src/components/SlotCard.tsx |
| 21 | When a group has no time clear of every member's placement, protected time, and life constraints, who should split off? | The student who blocks the most times gets a 1:1 conference of their own and the rest keep the workshop; a coordinator may prefer to move the placement instead. | src/engine/propose.ts |
| 22 | Should a Required need on a lesson with an open prerequisite become a proposal on the prerequisite (as now) or stay on the lesson with a note? | Redirected to the prerequisite, since the group then shares what it is missing; the card says where each student came from. | src/engine/propose.ts |
