## High

- **Coordinator placements carry a pathway edge the graph does not have, and 73 why lines on 24 students' weeks say a placement "builds toward" a pathway it is not on.** `src/engine/build.ts:133` treats any offering whose `source_ref` starts with `XT.` as on the student's Futre pathway (`|| o.source_ref.startsWith('XT.')`), writes the why `en.why.rwPathway` ("Paid work placement builds toward Journalism." for stu_010, a Launch student, five times this week), and stores a `leads_to` edge from the template to the pathway in the slot's trace (`build.ts:135`). The graph holds `member_of` edges from experience to pathway, never `leads_to` from a template to a pathway, so 144 of the 206 assigned real-world slot traces in the committed fixture hold an edge that does not exist, and 73 of the 185 "builds toward" whys name a pathway whose experience list and required outcomes do not include the placement's template. The `XT.` clause predates this PR, but Draft Three made the edge a stored trace and made the why's edge an acceptance criterion. Departs from `specs/06-ENGINE.md` Why Text ("Where the graph was walked, the why names the edge in plain words"), `specs/06-ENGINE.md` Traceability ("the graph edges walked"), and `specs/10-KNOWLEDGE-GRAPH.md` Sandbox Acceptance ("Every walk the engine performs is stored in the output's trace with the edges in order"). Scenario 23's edge pattern matches the words "builds toward" in any sentence, so it cannot catch a false edge. Scenario missing: every edge in every stored trace exists in the graph, and every placement why that names a pathway has a graph path from the template to that pathway.
- **Accepting a proposal that found no time publishes a conference with no adult, at Monday 9:15, in the first place in the list.** When `findTime` finds nothing, `src/engine/propose.ts` emits the proposal with `adult_id: null`, `day: dates[0]`, `start: BLOCKS[0].start`; `offeringFromProposal` then publishes it with `adult_ids: []` and `place_id: snap.places[0].id`, and `acceptProposal` (`src/store/actions.ts`) places every student into it. The card (`src/screens/expert/ProposedWorkshops.tsx:50` and `:64`) shows that Monday time and an enabled Accept beside the "pick one with Change" line. The seed ships three such cards this week for the math expert (audit rows 10, 13, 14: Layla Williams, Anaya Cruz, Miles Washington), on the demo script's Proposed Workshops step. Departs from `specs/06-ENGINE.md` Expert Agreement Rules 7 and 9 (a proposal with no time cannot be accepted from the card alone and must not be placeable as is), `specs/06-ENGINE.md` Rules for proposeWorkshops 3 ("Choose an adult"), and `specs/04-SUPPLY-PUBLISHING.md` Publishing Rules ("Place is required and checked against Place.capacity and adults_required"). Scenario missing: Accept on a proposal without a time is refused or routes through Change, and no published offering has an empty `adult_ids`.

- **The audit export repeats proposals and prints decline lines that mean nothing for next week.** `src/engine/audit.ts:95` pads this week's open proposals with `proposeWorkshops(snap, { weekStart: nextWeek })`; rows 16, 17, 18, and 20 of the committed `audit/engine-audit-2026-10-20.md` are rows 1, 2, 3, and 4 again with a new time, and every next-week row's "If declined" cell reads "self-directed practice; no block with room this week" because `fallbackFor` searches only the proposal week's published menu, which the seed never publishes for next week.
